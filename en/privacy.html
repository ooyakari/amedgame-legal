Legal basis

Data needed to provide the game: performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Error logs and abuse prevention: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). Notifications: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in your device or in-game settings. Purchase records: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Service providers

  • Supabase: database and servers; data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • RevenueCat: purchase validation (USA; EU Standard Contractual Clauses).
  • Apple and Google: stores, payments, optional sign-in and notification delivery (Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging).

They process data only on our behalf and for these purposes. We share data with no one else unless required by law.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings → Account → “Download my data”.
  • Deletion: Settings → Account → “Delete account”. Your account and game data are permanently deleted; your chat messages show “deleted player”; purchase records are kept de-identified for the statutory period.
  • Rectification, restriction, objection: write to info@giran.de. We reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: you may contact the data protection authority in your country.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Your age range is asked the first time you use a restricted feature; under 13, free-text chat, custom wall writings and message previews in notifications are disabled and only preset messages are available.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Game results are computed on the server, and every table has row-level access control.

If this policy changes, we will tell you in the app and on this page.
